# Frequently Asked Questions

## Account Issues

### How do I reset my password?
To reset your password:
1. Go to the login page
2. Click "Forgot Password"
3. Enter your email address
4. Check your inbox for the reset link
5. Follow the link to create a new password

### I can't log in to my account
If you're having trouble logging in:
- Make sure you're using the correct email address
- Check that Caps Lock is off
- Try resetting your password
- Clear your browser cache and cookies
- If the problem persists, contact support

### How do I update my email address?
To update your email:
1. Log in to your account
2. Go to Account Settings
3. Click "Change Email"
4. Enter your new email address
5. Verify the new email through the confirmation link

## Payment Issues

### How do I update my payment method?
To update your payment method:
1. Log in to your account
2. Go to Billing > Payment Methods
3. Click "Add Payment Method"
4. Enter your new card details
5. Set as default if desired

### Why was I charged twice?
Double charges can occur due to:
- Temporary authorization holds that appear as charges
- Browser refresh during payment processing
- Network timeouts causing retry attempts

If you see a duplicate charge, please contact support with your order number and we'll investigate.

### How do I request a refund?
To request a refund:
1. Log in to your account
2. Go to Order History
3. Find the order you want refunded
4. Click "Request Refund"
5. Select a reason and submit

Refunds are typically processed within 5-7 business days.

## Technical Issues

### The website is loading slowly
If the website is slow:
- Check your internet connection
- Clear your browser cache
- Try a different browser
- Disable browser extensions
- Try again in a few minutes

### I'm getting an error message
If you encounter an error:
- Note the exact error message
- Take a screenshot if possible
- Try refreshing the page
- Clear your browser cache
- Contact support with the error details
